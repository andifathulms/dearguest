# Minang — Gonjong Songket

**Intent.** A Minangkabau *baralek* in black lacquer, Minang red and gold: the
curved *gonjong* roof horns of the *rumah gadang*, carved *ukiran* (*kaluak paku*
spirals, *pucuak rabuang* triangles), red-and-gold *songket*, the draped
*langik-langik* canopy, and a brass *carano* with *sirih* and *pinang*.

**Art** (painted with Gemini from `PROMPT-MINANG-ASSETS.md`; masters in
`design/packs/minang/masters/`, slot files built by `scripts/build_pack_art.py minang`;
cut-outs keyed from cobalt with blue despill):

| Slot | Use |
|---|---|
| `cover-bg` (+ desktop) | the cover: gonjong roofline over a langik-langik swag, songket pillars, carano at the foot |
| `hero-top` / `hero-bottom` | the hero on phones: the langik-langik canopy; carano on a songket runner |
| `hero-bg` | the hero on wide screens (the desktop rumah gadang walls) |
| `photo-ring` | carved gold oval with a songket band and a gonjong crest, on the hero portrait and both profiles |
| `corner-tl` (mirrored to `corner-br`) | carved gonjong bracket with a songket swag, as a lintel over the closing |
| `particles` | melati, sirih, pinang, mawar petal, pucuak rabuang and songket-diamond sequins |
| page background | `texture.webp`, seamless black lacquer (in overrides.css) |

**Type.** Great Vibes for the couple's names and section titles; Gilda Display
for display lines; Libre Baskerville for body text, spaced labels and italic lines.

**Ornaments** drawn in code: a pucuak rabuang triangle under titles, a songket
diamond-chain divider, and an oval monogram frame with a gonjong crest.

**Cultural notes for a reviewer.** Gonjong, ukiran, songket, langik-langik and
carano belong to Minang wedding custom; none is a religious symbol. No people,
no suntiang worn by a figure.
