# Taman Sage

**Intent.** Fresh, airy watercolour greenery on white paper: eucalyptus, olive
and ferns with white garden roses, blush ranunculus and baby's breath. The
bright, modern counterpart to Floral (dark wine) and Javanese (ivory adat), and
the style most couples ask for.

**Art** (painted with Gemini from `PROMPT-SAGE-ASSETS.md`; masters in
`design/packs/sage/masters/`, slot files built by `scripts/build_pack_art.py sage`;
cut-outs keyed from cobalt with blue despill):

| Slot | Use |
|---|---|
| `cover-bg` (+ desktop) | the cover: a garland top-left, a cluster bottom-right |
| `hero-top` / `hero-bottom` | the hero on phones: a hanging swag; a low meadow of ferns |
| `hero-bg` | the hero on wide screens (the desktop painting) |
| `photo-ring` | an open oval wreath around the hero portrait and both profiles |
| `corner-tl` (mirrored to `corner-br`) | frames the closing words |
| `particles` | white rose petal, blush petal, eucalyptus leaf, olive leaf, baby's breath, sage leaf |
| `section-edge` | section art: a column of eucalyptus with blush and white roses and baby's breath, at the sides of the sections (smaller on phones, beside the wide script titles) |
| `section-garland` | section art: a eucalyptus and olive swag with blush and white roses, the divider between sections |
| `monogram-wreath` | section art: a eucalyptus ring with a crescent of white and blush roses at the bottom, around the initials |
| `card-corner` | section art: an L-shaped eucalyptus sprig with one blush rose, on the event and gift cards |
| `parallax-back` | depth (section prompt S5): a far olive grove in soft mist, standing above the hero's rose and fern bed; trails the scroll (`depth` -0.1). Phones only |
| `parallax-front` | depth (section prompt S6): hanging eucalyptus sprigs at the top corners, ahead of the scroll. Phones only |
| page background | `texture.webp`, seamless white watercolour paper (in overrides.css) |

**Type.** Allura for the couple's names and section titles; Marcellus (a light
Roman) for display lines; Jost (geometric sans) for body text and spaced labels.

**Colour.** Page `#fcfcf9`, ink deep sage-grey `#3d4741`, accent sage `#5e7a6e`,
with muted gold `#b89a5e` only in tiny ornament details.

No religious or regional motifs, so the pack suits every copy variant.
